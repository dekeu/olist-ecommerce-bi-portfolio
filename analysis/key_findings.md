# Key Findings and Recommendations

## Project Overview

This analysis examines delivered sales, customer reach, delivery performance, customer reviews, and product-category freight pressure in the Olist e-commerce dataset.

The available data covers approximately September 2016 through October 2018. All monetary values use the dollar symbol ($) for consistency with the dashboard.

---

## Finding 1 — Sales Are Concentrated in São Paulo

### Evidence

The dashboard shows approximately $13.22 million in total delivered GMV. São Paulo (SP) contributed approximately $5.07 million, representing about 38.4% of total delivered GMV.

### Meaning

São Paulo is the company’s largest market in this dataset. This concentration means overall sales performance is particularly sensitive to changes in customer demand and operational performance in São Paulo.

### Recommended Action

The business should monitor São Paulo’s sales trend separately while also investigating opportunities to grow other major markets such as Rio de Janeiro and Minas Gerais.

### Measure

Track the following indicators monthly:

- Delivered GMV by state
- Percentage of total GMV contributed by São Paulo
- GMV growth in the next-largest states
- Delivered order volume by state

---

## Finding 2 — Most Orders Were Delivered On Time, but Delivery Risk Remains

### Evidence

The overall on-time delivery rate was approximately 91.9%, meaning approximately 8.1% of delivered orders were late. Average delivery time was approximately 12.5 days.

### Meaning

Most customers received their orders on time, but late deliveries still represent a meaningful operational risk. Late deliveries may damage customer trust and negatively affect future purchasing decisions.

### Recommended Action

The operations team should investigate states, routes, carriers, and product categories associated with longer delivery times. High-volume markets with poor delivery performance should receive priority.

### Measure

Track the following indicators monthly:

- On-time delivery rate
- Late-delivery rate
- Average delivery days
- Late-delivery rate by state
- Number of severely delayed orders

---

## Finding 3 — Late Delivery Is Associated with Lower Review Scores

### Evidence

Orders delivered late received an average review score of approximately 2.6, while orders delivered on time received an average score of approximately 4.3. This is a difference of approximately 1.7 review points.

### Meaning

The result suggests a strong association between delivery reliability and customer satisfaction. Customers who receive late orders appear considerably less satisfied than customers who receive orders on time.

### Recommended Action

The business should provide proactive delay notifications, improve delivery estimates, and prioritize follow-up communication for customers experiencing severe delays.

### Measure

Track the following indicators:

- Average review score for late deliveries
- Average review score for on-time deliveries
- Difference in review scores between the two groups
- On-time delivery rate
- Customer complaints related to delivery delays

---

# Limitations

- The dataset is historical and covers approximately 2016–2018, including partial months and partial years.
- GMV represents product value and should not be interpreted as Olist revenue or profit.
- The dataset does not contain complete information about business costs, profit margins, refunds, marketing expenditure, or customer acquisition costs.
- The analysis identifies an association between late delivery and lower review scores, but it does not prove that late delivery directly caused the lower scores.
- Some states and product categories contain fewer orders than others, so percentages based on small groups should be interpreted carefully.
- The results describe this historical dataset and should not be presented as Olist’s current business performance.

---

# Conclusion

The analysis indicates that delivered GMV is concentrated in São Paulo, most delivered orders arrived on time, and late delivery is associated with substantially lower customer review scores.

The main business priority should be maintaining reliable delivery performance in high-volume markets while investigating freight pressure and growth opportunities across product categories and states.
